Hudson's Drive & Discover

4+3

Grown-ups

Hold the big green button to drive. The motorcycle stops at each animal. Tap the picture that matches.

A wrong picture wiggles, then the right one pulses, then it stays lit until it is tapped. Nothing is timed or scored. At the end, tap Play again.

Voice lines are files named in audio/voice/manifest.json. An empty line stays quiet. To use a family recording, add the mp3 and put its filename on that line. A file for you_got_it is played instead of sounds_good.
